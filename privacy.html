Privacy Policy

Joisto: Contractor Calculator · Last updated September 25, 2026

Joisto is a construction calculator. Everything you type and save stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your jobs, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Sizes you enter, calculation results, jobs and material lists.
  • Your settings: units, rounding, waste factor and trade.

No permissions

Joisto does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. It works fully offline.

PDF export

When you export a job, the PDF is created on your iPhone and shared only where you choose in the share sheet.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Joisto receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We may use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases; it never receives your jobs or measurements.

Analytics and tracking

Joisto does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Deleting your data

Swipe a job to delete it, remove a calculation with its × button, or delete Joisto from your iPhone to remove all of its data.

Children

Joisto is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
