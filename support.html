Common questions

  • How do I type feet and inches? The way you say it: 8' 10 1/2", 106.5, 3/4". The keyboard bar has ' " / and space keys.
  • Can I use metric? Yes. Settings → Units → Metric. You can still type 12' in metric mode.
  • Where is my waste factor? Settings → Extra material. It's added to every material count.
  • Restore purchases. Settings → Restore purchases, or on the Pro screen.
  • Cancel a subscription. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Joisto.
